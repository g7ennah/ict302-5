HP Pavilion 15-F Repair

The HP Pavilion 15-f series is a line of 15.6-inch laptops, including models with Intel and AMD processors, HD or FHD display options. The models feature up to 8GB of RAM, HDD, or SSD storage. Models in this lineup include the f200c, f305dx, f337nr, f337wm, and f387wm. This page contains repair and service information related to the HP Pavilion 15-F series.

Models 305DX, 337NR, 337WM, 387WM

Background: The HP 15 Notebook PC is an economical laptop part of HP’s 15-inch consumer laptop portfolio. It was built primarily for students, casual users, and professionals who required an economical yet functional computer for daily computing work.

This series features a range of model numbers with minor hardware configuration differences, including f305dx, f200c, f387wm, f337nr, f337wm, and f3387wm. The laptop features a 15.6-inch display with HD (1366x768) resolution and is powered by various AMD or Intel CPUs, depending on the version.

Additional typical features included 500GB to 1TB hard drives, 4GB to 8GB of RAM, and integrated graphics solutions like AMD Radeon R4/R5 or Intel HD Graphics, making it appropriate for light gaming, document processing, online browsing, and media streaming.

Identification: The HP Notebook 15 series has a sleek, modern appearance with a lightweight but robust plastic or aluminum frame, depending on the model. The HP 15 Notebook PC has a screen size of 15.6 inches. It features a 16:9 ultra-wide aspect ratio white light-emitting diode (WLED) display. The laptop is 38.56 cm (15.18 in) in width, 25.8 cm (10.15 in) in depth, and 2.26 cm (0.89 in) in thickness. The laptop weighs between 2.09 kg (4.61 lb) and 2.29 kg (5.05 lb), depending on the setup. The service tag at the bottom of the laptop has the model number. The laptop comes in a single color—black. The HP logo, a white display bezel, and a textured island-style keyboard are important visual indicators.
