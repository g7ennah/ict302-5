## Google Pixel Tablet – Screen Replacement##


**Background**:

The Google Pixel Tablet features a bonded LCD and digitizer assembly that can become damaged due to drops, pressure points, or manufacturing aging. Users typically replace the screen when facing cracked glass, unresponsive touch input, flickering, or dead display zones. Because the screen is adhered around the perimeter, careful separation is necessary to avoid bending the frame or damaging internal cables. 

**Difficulty Level:**  ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐/5

**Time Required:45-75 minutes**
 
**Identification:**
You can identify the Google Pixel Tablet by: 
    ~A centered rear camera at the top edge 
    ~A matte soft-touch finish 
    ~Three pogo pins for the charging dock on the back 
    ~Model numbers usually beginning with GTU8P or GWX9M printed on the back panel 

If your device does not have pogo pins or the camera is off-center, you may be holding a different Google or Android tablet model. 

**Overview:**

This guide explains how to remove the adhered display assembly and replace it safely. The Pixel Tablet uses strong perimeter adhesive, so controlled heat and slow separation are key to a successful repair. 
 
**Tools Required:**

    ~Anti-Clamp (or suction tool) 
    ~Plastic opening picks 
    ~Plastic cards 
    ~Phillips #00 screwdriver 
    ~Spudger 

**Parts Needed:**
    ~Replacement Google Pixel Tablet display assembly 
    ~Adhesive frame or liquid adhesive (optional but recommended) 

**⚠️Safety Warning⚠️:**
The Pixel Tablet screen is large and fragile. Do not lift one side much higher than the other — this can crack the glass or tear the digitizer cable. Always keep your tools flat and avoid inserting anything more than a few millimeters deep. 

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

**Step 1 — Apply the Anti-Clamp to Begin Separation**

What to do: 

Place the Anti-Clamp on the center of the front screen. Begin applying light, even pressure. The goal is to slightly lift the glass and create a hairline gap along the edges. Do not rush this step — allow the pressure to gradually loosen the adhesive. If you’re not using an Anti-Clamp, warm the perimeter with a hairdryer and use a suction cup instead. 

![Alt text](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1509395062183-67c5ad6faff9)

**Step 2 — Insert Opening Picks Around the Frame**

What to do: 
Once a small gap forms, slide a plastic pick into the edge. Carefully glide it around the perimeter of the tablet to cut through the adhesive. Work slowly — the screen adhesive is thick and may require reheating sections. Insert additional picks to keep areas from re-sealing as you move. 

![Alt text](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1518770660439-4636190af475)

**Step 3 — Gently Lift the Screen Assembly**
 
What to do: 
After freeing the adhesive around all sides, lift the screen slightly from one edge. Keep the screen low and parallel to the frame — lifting too high can strain the ribbon cables. If the screen resists, do not force it; reheat and re-slice the remaining adhesive. 

![Alt text](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1584270354949-1f3c6f5f71a8)

**Step 4 — Disconnect the Display and Digitizer Cables**

What to do: 
Use a spudger to open the display cable connectors. Lift each cable straight upward — avoid twisting motions. Once the connectors are detached, you can fully separate the old screen from the tablet body. 

![Alt text](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1581091012184-5ce3100f8911)

**Step 5 — Install and Test the New Screen Assembly**

What to do: 
Position the replacement screen near the frame and reconnect the display cables. Before sealing, power on the tablet to confirm touch and image quality. If everything works, shut the device off, apply new adhesive around the perimeter, and press the screen firmly into place.
