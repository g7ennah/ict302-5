## Apple Watch Battery Replacement ##

Apple Watch batteries gradually lose capacity because of frequent charging cycles. Over time, users may begin noticing shorter battery life, unexpected shutdowns, or swelling beneath the screen. Replacing the battery restores performance and prevents potential damage to the display caused by battery expansion. 

![Alt text](https://i.ebayimg.com/images/g/a3YAAOSwaaxjc8SQ/s-l1200.jpg)

**Identification**

To identify your Apple Watch model, flip the device over and examine the text surrounding the heart-rate sensor. The series number (Series 1–8, SE, etc.) is printed along the rim. Earlier models have slightly thicker bezels and smaller displays, while modern versions have rounded edges and a larger screen area. 

**Difficulty Level:**  ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐/5

**Time Required:45-60minutes**

 
**Overview:**
This guide explains how to safely open the Apple Watch, detach delicate display cables, access the internal battery, and replace it with a new one. 

**Tools Required:**

    ~iOpener or heat gun 
    ~Fine tweezers 
    ~Small Phillips screwdriver 
    ~Plastic spudger 

**Parts Needed:**

    ~Replacement Apple Watch battery 
    ~Pre-cut adhesive gasket for resealing 

**⚠️Safety Warning⚠️:**

Handle the display gently — it is thin and fragile. Apply heat gradually and avoid bending or twisting the display panel. 

**🛠 Step-by-Step Instructions:**

**Step 1 — Warm the Display Perimeter**

What to Do: 
Use an iOpener or heat gun on a low setting to warm the edges of the watch face. Move the heat source in slow circles, ensuring you don’t concentrate heat in one place. The goal is to soften the strong adhesive holding the display to the metal frame. 


**Step 2 — Pry Up the Display**

![Alt text](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1527430253228-e93688616381)

What to Do: 
Once the adhesive softens, place a thin blade or plastic pick beneath one edge of the screen. Lift gently, applying just enough upward motion to separate the glass from the case. Work around the perimeter with small motions until the screen loosens and can hinge upward. 


**Step 3 — Disconnect Touch & Display Cables**

What to Do: 
Carefully open the screen like a book. Inside, you’ll see two small ribbon cables connecting the display and touch layer to the motherboard. Use tweezers or a spudger to unlock the tiny connectors. Detach each cable slowly to prevent tearing. 

**Step 4 — Access the Battery**

![Alt text](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1518770660439-4636190af475)

What to Do: 
Once the display is detached, the battery becomes visible in the center of the watch. Remove the shield plate held in place with a small screw. Lift the plate and set it aside. This gives access to the battery connector beneath it. 

**Step 5 — Remove the Old Battery**

What to Do: 
Use a plastic spudger to lift the battery from its adhesive. Be patient — Apple uses strong adhesive inside the watch. If the battery doesn’t lift easily, apply a small amount of controlled heat from the underside of the watch to loosen the glue. Do not puncture the battery.  

**Step 6 — Install the New Battery**

What to Do: 
Place the new battery into the housing and connect it to the motherboard. Reinstall the shield plate and verify the battery cable sits flat. Before sealing, briefly reconnect the display and turn on the watch to confirm proper functionality. 
If everything works, apply a new adhesive gasket, close the display, and press firmly around the edges until sealed.
