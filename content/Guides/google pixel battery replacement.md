## Google Pixel Battery Replacement

The Google Pixel (1st generation) uses a lithium-ion battery that degrades over time. Common symptoms include rapid battery drain, random shutdowns, and swollen battery cells. Replacing the battery restores device longevity and prevents potential damage caused by battery swelling. 

**Difficulty Level:**  ⭐⭐⭐/5

**Time Required:30–45 minutes**

**Identification:**
You can identify this device by its: 
    ~Two-tone aluminum and glass back design 
    ~Single rear camera 
    ~Circular fingerprint sensor centered on the back 
    ~Model number “G-2PW4100” printed on the bottom rear side 
    ~If your device matches these features, you have the correct guide. 

**Overview:** 
This guide explains how to remove the screen, disconnect internal components, and safely install a new battery. 

**Tools Required:**

    ~Heat gun or hairdryer 
    ~Suction cup 
    ~Plastic opening picks 
    ~Phillips #00 screwdriver 
    ~Plastic spudger 

**Parts Needed:**

    ~Replacement Google Pixel battery 
    ~Adhesive strips or double-sided adhesive tape 

 
**⚠️Safety Warning⚠️:**

    ~Do not pull or twist the battery if it resists — reheat the adhesive. 
    ~Do not use metal tools near the battery. 
    ~The Pixel display is fragile; too much heat or pressure can crack it. 

📘**Step-by-Step Instructions (FULL DESCRIPTIONS):**

**Step 1 — Heat the Display**

![Alt text](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1519389950473-47ba0277781c)

What to Do: 
Use a heat gun or hairdryer and apply heat evenly around all four edges of the Pixel’s display. Move the heat source in slow circles to avoid overheating one area. 

Purpose: 
The Pixel’s display is secured with strong adhesive. Heating softens this adhesive, allowing you to lift the screen without cracking it. 

Tips: 
Heat for 1–2 minutes 
The surface should be warm, not burning hot 
If the glass is too hot to touch, stop and let it cool slightly 

**Step 2 — Insert Opening Picks:**

![Alt text](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1581092334714-1d4c6c27b8d5)

What to Do: 
~Place a suction cup near the bottom edge of the phone. 
~Gently pull upward to create a slight gap between the display and the frame. 
~Slide a thin opening pick into the gap and slowly work around the perimeter. 

Purpose: 
The picks cut through the softened adhesive that holds the screen in place. 

Tips: 
~Keep the pick parallel to the screen 
~Never insert deeper than 3–4 mm 
~If resistance increases, reheat and continue 

**Step 3 — Disconnect the Display Cable**

![Alt text](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1584270354949-1f3c6f5f71a8)

What to Do: 
~Lift the screen slightly from the right side like a book cover. Do NOT pull it upward. 
~Inside, you will see a small ribbon cable connecting the display to the motherboard. 
~Use a plastic spudger to gently pop the display cable connector upward. 

Purpose: 
Disconnecting ensures you don’t damage the cable when handling the screen. 

Tips: 
~Do not pull the display away abruptly 
~Support the display while unplugging 
~Avoid touching exposed circuitry with your fingers 

**Step 4 — Remove the Midframe Shield**

![Alt text](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1555617981-dac3880fedd5)

What to Do: 
Locate the metal midframe plate covering the battery connector. 
Use a Phillips #00 screwdriver to remove the screws securing the plate. 
Lift the plate with tweezers or a spudger. 

Purpose: 
This shield protects the internal connectors. Removing it gives access to the battery cable. 

Tips: 
Keep screws organized — they vary in length 
Do not bend the shield 
Use minimal force when lifting 

**Step 5 — Remove the Battery**

![Alt text](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1584974293023-7c0cbb3596d7)

What to Do: 
Disconnect the battery connector by prying upward with a spudger. 
Locate the pull tabs under the battery and pull them slowly to release adhesive. 
If the pull tab breaks: 
Apply gentle heat to the back of the phone 
Insert a plastic card under the battery to loosen adhesive 

Purpose: 
Removing the battery allows you to install a fresh, healthy one. 

Tips: 
Never bend, pierce, or deform the battery 
If the battery feels stuck, reheat — don’t force it 
Avoid using metal tools 

**Step 6 — Install the New Battery**

![Alt text](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1555617117-08fda8a6f72b)

What to Do: 
Place new adhesive in the battery tray. 
Set the replacement battery into position and reconnect the battery cable. 
Reinstall the midframe shield and screws. 
Reconnect the display cable, align the screen, and press firmly around the edges to seal. 

Purpose: 
Ensures your new battery is securely mounted and the phone reassembles correctly. 

Tips: 
Test the phone before fully sealing the screen 
If the screen doesn’t sit flush, check for cable obstruction 
Apply steady pressure for 30 seconds to bond the adhesive
