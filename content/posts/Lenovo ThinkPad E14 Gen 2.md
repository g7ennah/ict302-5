**Background:**
The Lenovo ThinkPad E14 Gen 2 is a business laptop made for everyday use, offering a mix of performance, reliability, and portability. The Lenovo ThinkPad E14 Gen 2 (model number: 20TA/20TB) was released in 2020 as part of Lenovo’s ThinkPad E series, which is designed for students, professionals, and small businesses. It’s built to handle daily tasks like writing, web browsing, video meetings, and presentations while keeping the classic ThinkPad durability.

The ThinkPad E14 Gen 2 has a 14-inch Full HD anti-glare screen and runs on either AMD Ryzen 4000 series or Intel 11th Gen Core processors. It supports up to 32GB of memory and offers both solid-state (SSD) and hard drive (HDD) storage options. The laptop includes a variety of ports—such as USB-C, USB 3.2, and HDMI—and supports Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. Other useful features include a fingerprint reader for security, a spill-resistant keyboard, and a built-in webcam with a privacy cover.

The laptop’s exterior is made from aluminum or durable black plastic, weighing around 3.5 pounds. Its simple, professional design fits well in both school and office settings. Users appreciate its long battery life, solid build, and smooth performance. There have been no major recalls for this model, though some users report light fan noise or heat during heavy use.

The ThinkPad E14 Gen 2 was later replaced by the ThinkPad E14 Gen 3, which offered faster processors and better graphics. Still, the E14 Gen 2 remains a popular and dependable option for anyone who needs a sturdy, well-performing laptop at a reasonable price.

**Identification:**
The Lenovo ThinkPad Gen 2 features a slim, black frame with the iconic red TrackPoint centered between the G, H, and B keys on the keyboard. The power button is located on the top right corner above the keyboard. You’ll find the model and serial number on a sticker on the bottom of the device, alongside the Lenovo and ThinkPad branding. The ports are split between the left and right edges, with ventilation grilles usually on the left side. A distinguishing feature of this device is the illuminated ThinkPad logo on the lid and palm rest. This model is only available in classic ThinkPad black.
