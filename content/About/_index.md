Our team formed around a simple belief that everyone should have the ability and the freedom to repair the products they rely on. Many of us had faced the frustration of trying to fix something without the right information or support, and those shared experiences inspired our mission.

What began as a small group of problem-solvers and advocates has grown into a collective effort focused on making repair knowledge accessible and easy to understand. We believe this information should not be restricted or difficult to find. Anyone who wants to learn should feel empowered to take on repairs with confidence.

What guides us is the commitment to share knowledge openly so that people can take control of their own repairs.
